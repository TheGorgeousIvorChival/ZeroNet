### What this says we should ship

* **ChaCha20: ship the ladder on this platform.**  
  The gate above fails the job on a single length below 1.00x, and this run produced none, on any of the four runners in the matrix. iOS and Android are the same ISA as the two aarch64 runners and are compile-gated rather than guessed at.
* **SHA-1: ship it here.**  
  The portable core's worst length here is 1.74x. ZeroNet's only SHA-1 is the 55-byte WebSocket handshake, so this is the number that matters.
* **SHA-256: bump the pin to `sha2` 0.11, not to the portable core.**  
  Its hardware backend measures 5.81x at its worst here against the 0.10 pin ZeroNet resolves today, and the portable core only 1.78x. Where the CPU already had those instructions it is 1.00x, so it is a strict improvement on every platform and a dependency bump rather than a new core.
