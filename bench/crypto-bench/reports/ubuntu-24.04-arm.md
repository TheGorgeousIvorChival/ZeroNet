### What this says we should ship

* **ChaCha20: ship the ladder here.** The gate above fails the job on a single
  length below 1.00x, and this run produced none, on any of the four runners in
  the matrix. iOS and Android are the same ISA as the two aarch64 runners and
  are compile-gated rather than guessed at.
* **SHA-1: ship it on this platform.** The portable core's worst length here is 1.74x. ZeroNet's only SHA-1 is the 55-byte WebSocket handshake, so this is thenumber that matters.
* **SHA-256: bump the pin to `sha2` 0.11, not to the portable core.** Thehardware backend measures 5.83x at its worst here against the 0.10 pinZeroNet resolves today, and the portable core only 1.79x. Where the CPUalready had the instructions it is 1.00x, so it is a strict improvementeverywhere and a dependency bump rather than a new core.
