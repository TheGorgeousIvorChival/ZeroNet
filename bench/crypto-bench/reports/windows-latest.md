### What this says we should ship

* **ChaCha20: ship the ladder on this platform.**  
  The gate above fails the job on a single length below 1.00x, and this run produced none, on any of the four runners in the matrix. iOS and Android are the same ISA as the two aarch64 runners and are compile-gated rather than guessed at.
* **SHA-1: do not ship it here.**  
  The portable core's worst length here is 0.37x. The crate's SHA-NI is the right answer on this CPU and the aarch64 reports are where the portable core belongs. Shipping it here would slow the handshake down for no reason.
* **SHA-256: leave the pin alone here.**  
  The hardware backend in `sha2` 0.11 measures 0.95x at its worst here against the 0.10 pin ZeroNet resolves today; the portable core measures 0.16x. That is a real, if small, loss and it is not noise-shaped: it is the runtime dispatch, on a platform that already had SHA-NI. But note what the same column reads on the other runners: this is a dependency bump, not a new core, and the win is all aarch64 -- SHA-NI was already there on x86_64 and 0.11's runtime dispatch costs a few percent it never gives back.
